Railhook sends your product's webhooks and receives the ones it depends on, retries what fails and records every attempt.

- [Quickstart](/docs/start/quickstart/): from nothing to a delivered webhook.
- [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting/overview/): what an installation runs and what it needs.
- [API reference](/docs/api-reference/): every endpoint, with requests you can send to your instance.

Railhook Cloud runs at [railhook.io](https://railhook.io/register), free to start. To run it on your own server (about 4 GiB of RAM):

```bash title="Install on your server"
curl -fsSL https://railhook.io/install.sh | bash
```
