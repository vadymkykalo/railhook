## Railhook

- **Both directions.** Outgoing events to customer endpoints and incoming provider webhooks share one attempt lifecycle, one dashboard and one set of failed messages.
- **Everything self-hosted.** Every feature is in the MIT repository, with no licence key. `BILLING_ENABLED` defaults to `false`.
- **Railhook Cloud** runs at railhook.io on a free plan. Paid plans with support and higher limits will come later.
- **Standard Webhooks signatures** alongside Railhook's own `X-Signature` header, with a grace window on secret rotation.
- **Per-endpoint ordering**, opt-in, with a timeout so one stuck delivery cannot block an endpoint forever.
- **Replay creates new deliveries** from stored events (Time Machine in the dashboard) instead of re-sending the original.
- **A built-in request bin and a localhost tunnel** in the CLI.
- **A workflow builder** for multi-step processing beyond single transformations.
- **An embeddable customer portal**, self-hosted: your users register endpoints, choose event types, see their deliveries and retry failed ones. Sessions are short-lived and can be pinned to the embedding origin. See [Customer portal](/docs/outgoing/customer-portal/).

### What Railhook does not have

- **A young customer portal.** Theming is a brand colour, a logo, light or dark, and the language. No custom CSS, no custom portal domain, no view of the event catalog's schemas.
- **HTTP delivery only.** No SQS, S3, EventBridge, Pub/Sub, RabbitMQ or Kafka destinations.
- **No SSO (SAML or OIDC) and no SCIM.**
- **No OpenTelemetry.** Prometheus metrics are thorough; there are no traces.
- **Three fixed roles and two API key scopes.** No custom roles, no per-resource permissions.
- **Filtering is project-level.** A subscription cannot carry its own filter, and incoming webhooks cannot be filtered.
- **No automatic disabling** of an endpoint that keeps failing.
- **No batching, no built-in static egress IPs** (see [Static egress IP](/docs/resources/static-egress-ip/)), no PagerDuty or Opsgenie alert channel.
- **SDKs for Node.js, Python and PHP only.** No Go SDK and no Terraform provider.

## Svix

- The Svix server is open source under the MIT licence and can export traces to an OpenTelemetry collector. ([svix/svix-webhooks](https://github.com/svix/svix-webhooks))
- Its App Portal lets your customers add endpoints, debug deliveries, and inspect and replay past webhooks. ([App Portal](https://docs.svix.com/app-portal)) Svix states the portal is included only in the hosted version, not the open-source one. ([Consumer App Portal (OSS)](https://docs.svix.com/app-portal/oss))
- Svix Ingest receives webhooks from providers, verifies them and forwards them to destinations. ([Receiving with Ingest](https://docs.svix.com/ingest/receiving-with-ingest))

**Choose Svix** if a polished self-service customer portal is the priority and a hosted service suits you.

## Hookdeck

- **Outpost** is Hookdeck's open-source, Apache-2.0 product for sending events. It delivers to webhooks, Hookdeck Event Gateway, Amazon EventBridge, AWS SQS, AWS S3, GCP Pub/Sub, RabbitMQ and Kafka, and includes a user portal and OpenTelemetry traces, metrics and logs. ([hookdeck/outpost](https://github.com/hookdeck/outpost))
- **Event Gateway** receives webhooks at a source URL and routes them through connections to destinations, with filters, transformations, deduplication and retries. ([Hookdeck docs](https://hookdeck.com/docs/sources))

**Choose Hookdeck** if your customers' receivers are queues or cloud event buses rather than HTTP services. Railhook cannot deliver there.

## Convoy

- Convoy sends webhooks to your users and receives webhooks from providers. It is licensed under the Elastic License 2.0. ([frain-dev/convoy](https://github.com/frain-dev/convoy))
- Some features of the self-hosted edition are enabled by a licence key, and the free Community edition is limited to one user, one organisation and two projects. ([frain-dev/convoy](https://github.com/frain-dev/convoy))
- Portal Links generate a customer-facing dashboard of an endpoint's deliveries, as a one-time link or one you embed. ([Portal Links](https://getconvoy.io/docs/product-manual/portal-links))

**Choose Convoy** if you want both directions and an embeddable delivery view, and its licence terms fit.

## Hook0

- Hook0 is a service for sending webhooks to your customers. It is licensed under the Server Side Public License, and its hosted service runs in the EU, in France by default. ([Hook0](https://www.hook0.com/pricing))

**Choose Hook0** if a hosted sending service must keep its data in the EU and its licence terms fit.
